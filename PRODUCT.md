# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students preparing for classes who need to turn course material into recall practice and decide what to review next.

## Product Purpose

Abii is an active-recall study app. It helps students make flashcards, test their knowledge, organize study material, and understand which topics need another review.

## Positioning

Abii makes a student actively retrieve an answer instead of only rereading course material, within a friendly study space.

## Operating Context

The public site introduces prospective students to a study product. Authenticated users organize subjects, folders, topics, and flashcards, then use review and progress workflows.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The public homepage must explain Abii, its value, its study feel, and the next step. It may offer a non-persistent interactive recall demonstration. Only the Home concept is in scope; Features, About, Blog, and Guides are future destinations. Authentication routes `/login` and `/register` remain live. The preview route must return not-found in production.

## Brand Commitments

Friendly education, serious product. Existing Abii mascot assets preserve the mascot identity. Use Cute Dino only for the Abii wordmark and Onest otherwise. The existing white and pink semantic palette is binding.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing mascot images live in `public/mascot/`; product imagery lives in `public/dashboard-hero/` and `public/abii-head.png`. The concept uses clearly identified illustrative UI where a product screen is not selected.

## Product Principles

- Put active recall before passive rereading.
- Keep studying organized and understandable.
- Make the product feel encouraging without overstating outcomes.
- Keep the first visit focused on starting a study habit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public experience must be keyboard operable, support clear focus states, have a responsive mobile composition, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
